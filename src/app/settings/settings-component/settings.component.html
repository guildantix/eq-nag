Death recap

The death recap window can give you a "bird's eye view" of what happened leading up to a character's demise.

Select how you would like the death recap window to be loaded.

Off
Hotkey
Automatic

Off disables the Death Recap trigger. Hotkey opens the window when your EQ hotkey says “wonders how they died”. Automatic opens it when you're slain.

To create a hotkey, copy the command below and paste it into EverQuest and hit enter.

{{deathRecapPreferences.hotkeyPhrase}}

What the window shows

The Damage Log section will display what happened to your character, including damage taken and healing received.
The Damage Sources section will show a list of all NPC damage done to the raid, and all healing received by the raid.
The Encounter Statistics will list the general statistics of the raid, and a list of metrics for each enemy encountered in the fight.

Push recap Advanced

The push recap window can tell you who pushed the boss. After a fight, it reads your log backwards to the last boss kill and ranks every player by the number of push events, with the spells behind them.
Nothing runs while you fight. The log is only read when you press the hotkey.

Nag has a built-in Push Recap trigger. Make an EQ Hotkey that when pressed, will display the recap of your last boss kill.
Turn on other players' hits and spell messages in EverQuest to see the whole raid. The spell data is read from the EverQuest install that holds your character's log, or else from the EverQuest folder Nag found.
Hits are counted from spell damage messages like "... points of ... damage by <spell>.". Other players' spell damage messages must be on in EverQuest's chat filters to see their hits.

To create a hotkey, copy the command below and paste it into EverQuest and hit enter.

{{ pushRecapHotkeyCommand }}
