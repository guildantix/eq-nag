The colors match the app's Regex Visualizer, except that the Visualizer shows all groups in gold. Here each capture slot has its own color so you can follow it from the phrase to the log line to the table. The tester does not apply Speak's pronunciation changes, so Speak may say some words differently, for example roman numerals.
